Our story

Every stitch has a story

The Yarn Basket is a small crochet studio run with a lot of love and a lot of yarn. Every piece is made by hand — one loop, one stitch, one colour at a time — so no two are ever exactly alike.

From cuddly toys and cosy wearables to home décor and gifts, everything in the basket is made to be held, gifted and kept. Have an idea of your own? Tell us the colours and we’ll stitch it.
